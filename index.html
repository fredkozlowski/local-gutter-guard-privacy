Privacy Policy

Local Gutter Guard · Effective October 25, 2026

Who we are

Local Gutter Guard connects homeowners in Allegheny, Westmoreland, Butler, Washington and Beaver counties (Pittsburgh, PA area) with local gutter guard installers for free, no-obligation quotes.

What we collect

When you submit a quote request through one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, we collect the information you enter in the form: your county, the number of stories of your home, whether you already have a gutter guard quote, and your phone number.

How we use it

  • To match your request with a gutter guard installer who serves your area and to have them contact you by phone or text about a quote.
  • To follow up with you about your request.
  • To measure how well our ads work, in aggregate.

Who we share it with

We share your form answers and phone number with one or more licensed local gutter guard installers so they can contact you with a quote. We do not sell your information to data brokers, and we do not share it with anyone else except as required by law or to operate our systems (for example, our spreadsheet and messaging providers).

Retention

We keep quote requests for up to 12 months, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Your choices

You may ask us to delete your information, stop contacting you, or tell you what we hold about you. To do so, message our Facebook Page or email us at the address below. Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts.

Facebook and Instagram

Our quote forms run on Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram). Meta's handling of your data is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
